Concept Project

Stella Fit — Landing Page & Marketing Concept

Role: Designer & Developer Tools: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Canva, Claude AI
Live Demo ↗

The Idea

A fictional personal training brand created to explore branding, landing-page design, and audience-focused messaging. The goal was to build a clean, approachable page that encourages prospective clients to take the low-friction first step of booking a free discovery call.

My Approach

  • Created Visual Identity: Selected a warm, approachable color palette and clean typography to communicate wellness without the intimidating tone of traditional gym marketing.
  • Structured Landing Page Content: Used a clear Problem–Agitate–Solution (PAS) framework: acknowledging common fitness frustrations, presenting supportive personal training as the solution, and providing clear program details.
  • Designed a Focused CTA: Positioned "Book a Discovery Call" as a zero-pressure entry point with reassuring micro-copy ("15 minutes • No obligation").
  • Trust Signals & Social Proof: Designed testimonial card layouts and client progress highlights to illustrate how credibility is built on a service page.
  • Planned A/B Testing Concept: Formulated an A/B test plan comparing headline and button copy ("Book Your Discovery Call" vs. "Start Your Journey") to evaluate clarity versus emotional appeal.
  • Planned Analytics Events: Defined specific custom event triggers in GA4 to measure button click-through rate, scroll percentage, and time spent on key sections.

Marketing Thinking

Service-based landing pages must quickly establish psychological safety. For a female-focused personal training offer, high-pressure sales copy often increases friction. Instead, the messaging was crafted around empowerment and sustainable habits, answering common hesitations directly before presenting the booking form.

Planned Measurement Framework

Metrics that would be tracked:

  • CTA Click Rate: Percentage of unique visitors clicking the primary discovery call button.
  • Scroll Depth: Measuring how many visitors reach the program details and pricing breakdown.
  • Form Interaction Rate: Tracking form start versus form completion to identify potential input friction.

Note: In this concept project, testing and analytics represent the planning framework and event structure rather than live, statistically significant traffic data.

Website Concept & Audience Messaging

DropSwift is a fictional SaaS platform that automates order processing and supplier tracking for ecommerce store owners. The project demonstrates how landing-page messaging connects to post-signup email nurturing:

  • Landing Page: Addresses the core pain point of ecommerce founders: spending repetitive hours on order fulfillment instead of marketing and scaling.
  • Audience Segmentation: Structured copy to speak to both new solo founders (simplicity & time savings) and growing stores (error reduction & scaling capacity).
  • Conversion Pathways: Highlighted a simple 14-day trial signup with clear feature breakdowns, automated ROI calculator concept, and FAQ section.

Three-Email Post-Signup Sequence

To explore customer retention and email sequence design, I mapped out and designed three targeted email templates:

  • Email 1 (Day 0 — Welcome & Quick Win): Welcomes the user, reinforces the decision to sign up, and walks through connecting their first store in under 3 minutes.
  • Email 2 (Day 3 — Overcoming Complexity): Addresses the primary hesitation ("Will this disrupt my existing store?") by showcasing how simple sync rules work and offering a setup checklist.
  • Email 3 (Day 7 — Conversion & Upgrade): Highlights the productivity hours saved during the trial and provides a clear incentive to upgrade before trial expiration.

Note: This sequence is a creative and strategic communication concept; no actual conversion rates or commercial metrics are fabricated.

Brand Overview & Target Audience

The Brand: Aarya Coastal Retreat & Villas is a fictional boutique eco-luxury resort situated along Sri Lanka's serene southern coastline near Mirissa. It emphasizes tropical minimalism, restorative wellness, organic Ceylon culinary experiences, and unhurried coastal living.

Target Audience:

  • Digital Nomads & Remote Creatives: Individuals looking for inspiring work-friendly spaces with high-speed connectivity and calming ocean views.
  • Local Weekend Escapists: Professionals in Sri Lanka seeking premium, private staycation retreats.

Project Overview

CookMate is an Android culinary application developed as a team project for our university coursework. The mobile app helps users discover recipes based on dietary restrictions, ingredients on hand, and personal allergy preferences.

My Technical Contributions

  • User Authentication: Implemented Firebase Authentication with email/password and Google Sign-In.
  • Secure Password Reset: Engineered a secure email OTP password reset mechanism with time-limited codes in Firestore and anti-enumeration security, preventing user account discovery.
  • Database Schema & Preferences: Designed Firestore document models for user profiles, saved favorites, allergy filters, and ratings/reviews.
  • Team Collaboration: Collaborated via Git and GitHub with pull requests, code reviews, and structured branch management.
